About Jimmy's Landscaping

We are a local landscaping business. We work on gardens, lawns and outdoor spaces for homes and businesses, and we do the work properly the first time.

Every property is different, so we start by looking at what you have and what you want from it. From there we agree on the work, the approach and the cost before anything begins — no surprises once we are on site.

What you can expect from us is careful workmanship, clear communication and a team that turns up when we say we will.
